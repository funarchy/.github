## Funarchy

A workers' cooperative being founded to build open-source tools for
people creating together: cooperative workflows, education, and
governance instruments for communities that want to decide their own
rules.

Five principles, encoded rather than decorated — openness,
non-exploitation, sovereignty, permissionlessness, co-benefit. They are
the one thing that cannot be amended: a Funarchy that wants different
values forks instead of amending.

The defining design decision: **founder power is built to dissolve.**
Governance moves from founder-led, to federated domains, to a community
assembly — on stated triggers, not on anyone's goodwill. The documents
are written so the organization outgrows its founder by design rather
than by accident.

Work happens problem-first and in the open. Anyone may report a
**pollution** — a problem described as a problem, never a prescribed
fix — and anyone may propose a **solution**: the exact new text.
Membership is required for neither.

## The open seat

I'm looking for a **partner** — not an employee, not an investor, an
equal. 2-of-2 from the very beginning: strategic decisions require both
of us, the treasury requires both keys, and if we disagree the change
simply does not happen. Capital is not the ask; shared authority and the
operational half of the organization are.

## Work in progress

None of this is finished. The charter and the product documents are
reviewed line by line and stand behind every sentence; the roadmap,
bylaws, treasury, and onboarding documents are drafts and should be read
as drafts. The cooperative is not yet registered.

**[funarchy/org](https://github.com/funarchy/org)** holds all of it —
charter, the seven-product stack, roadmap, amendment process, and the
public record of how any of it changes.
